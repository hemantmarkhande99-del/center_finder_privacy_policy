1. Overview

Center Finder is an Android tool. Point the camera at a part, a hole, or a group of similar items. The app outlines the largest shape and marks its center, or counts items in an area you choose.

Satnamapps (“we”, “us”) publishes the app. This policy covers the Android app with package name com.satnamapps.shapecenterfinder, version 1.0 and later versions that keep the same data practices. If a later version starts uploading photos or adding an account, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Short version. The camera is required. Frames are processed on the device and then discarded. We do not run a Center Finder account system, we do not show ads, and we do not sell personal information.

3. What the app does

  • Center Finder. A live camera overlay finds the largest object, draws its outline, names a rough shape, and marks the geometric center.
  • Object Counter. You capture the current camera view in the app, drag a box if you want, and the app counts similar items. The picture stays in memory for that session.

Both tools need the camera. If you deny camera permission, the app cannot do its job and shows that the camera is required.

5. Camera and on-device vision

The camera is the core feature. This is why the app asks for it when it opens.

  • Center finding uses the camera preview and analyzes the latest frame on the device with OpenCV.
  • Counting can keep one snapshot in memory. Crops of detected items are made only to compare shape and appearance, then released.
  • Appearance matching uses MediaPipe Tasks Vision and a MobileNet v3 Small model that is packaged inside the app (mobilenet_v3_small.tflite). The model is not downloaded per photo, and the photo is not sent to a model API.
  • Closing the screen or the app drops those in-memory images. The app does not build a photo library.

We do not use the camera in the background. There is no background camera service. The camera hardware is required; a device without a camera cannot run the tool.

The app does not request photo-library access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or storage read), location, microphone, contacts, SMS, phone state, or the advertising ID.

Android backup is left at the platform default. The app does not write photos, counts, or account records into storage, so there is no personal content of that kind for backup to copy.

8. Libraries on the device

These components run inside the app. They are not a separate Center Finder account.

  • CameraX — camera preview and analysis.
  • OpenCV — edges, contours, center, and counting on the device.
  • MediaPipe Tasks Vision — on-device image embedding for similar-object matching, using the bundled MobileNet model (Apache License 2.0).
  • Android Jetpack — Compose UI and navigation. No extra personal-data collection is configured.

Firebase, Google Sign-In, AdMob, and Play Billing are not part of this app.

9. Retention and deletion

Camera frames and the count snapshot live in memory for the session. They are not stored in an account, because there is no account. We do not keep a server copy, because we do not receive one.

To remove anything still on the device, force-stop Center Finder or clear the app’s cache and storage in Android Settings. Uninstalling the app removes the app and its local data.

If you still want a deletion request on record, email hemantmarkhande590@gmail.com with the subject “Center Finder data deletion”. We will confirm that we hold no account or photo for you. This page is also the public URL for that request.

10. Children

Center Finder is a general workshop and measurement tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not include social features, chat, or user profiles.

11. Security

Because camera images are not transmitted to us, the main protection is that they stay on your device under Android’s normal app sandbox. Keep your device locked and install the app only from Google Play or another source you trust.

No method of handling data is perfect. This policy describes the practices of the app as built. It is not a promise that a modified, sideloaded, or unofficial copy behaves the same way.
