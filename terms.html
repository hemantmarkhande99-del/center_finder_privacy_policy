2. The app

Center Finder is a camera tool. It marks the geometric center of the largest shape it detects, and it counts discrete items in a view or in a box you draw. Results depend on light, contrast, focus, and how much objects overlap. A count or a center mark can be wrong.

You are responsible for checking a result before you drill, cut, or rely on a count. The app is an aid, not a calibrated measuring instrument.
